# Community standards

We want ServerSage to be welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

Be respectful, explain disagreements without personal attacks, and help keep discussions useful and accessible. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. Report a concern privately through the repository's contact or security reporting options.
